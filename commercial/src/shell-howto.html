    What is running in here

    No libraries, no video file, no stock footage. Every frame is computed at runtime: a hand-written 3D pipeline (matrix math, ear-clipping triangulation, painter-sorted rasterisation, Blinn-Phong shading) feeding a 2D canvas.

    The phone and the dashboard are perspective-correct textured quads — the app UI is drawn to an off-screen canvas, then mapped onto 3D geometry subdivided in world space, so it tracks the surface exactly. Acts II and III show real screenshots of the shipped app, panned and lit region by region as the voice-over names each part.

    Post chain: bloom, RGB split on cuts, film grain, vignette, scanlines, colour grade. The score is synthesised in WebAudio — kick, sub-bass, hats, plucks, pads, risers and trailer impacts, arranged at 120 BPM in C minor with every impact on an act cut. The kit drops out at 59.4 s so the closing line lands in near-silence.

    Hotkeys: space play · ←/→ step one frame · R restart · M mute · C captions · V 9:16.

    Delivery

    16:9 for YouTube pre-roll, CTV, website hero and paid social in-feed. 9:16 (press V) re-frames the same timeline for Reels, TikTok and Shorts with a brand bar and a persistent CTA.

    Captions are burned in by default — most feed views are watched with the sound off. The film ships with the score only; node commercial/render.mjs --script writes the timed voice-over sheet and an .srt, and --vo take3.wav muxes a recorded read with the music side-chained underneath it.

    Render a distributable file with node commercial/render.mjs: it steps the timeline frame-by-frame in headless Chromium, bounces the score to WAV, and muxes an H.264 MP4 with ffmpeg. Flags: --vertical, --fps 60, --no-captions, --vo <file>, --script, --gif.

    Everything sales-facing — brand, tagline, CTA, URL, trust badges, disclaimer, and the caption track — lives in one OTA_CONFIG block at the top of the file.
