    What is running in here

    No libraries, no video file, no stock footage. Every frame is computed at runtime: a hand-written 3D pipeline (matrix math, ear-clipping triangulation, painter-sorted rasterisation, Blinn-Phong shading) feeding a 2D canvas.

    The phone and the dashboard are perspective-correct textured quads — the app UI is drawn to an off-screen canvas, then mapped onto 3D geometry subdivided in world space, so it tracks the surface exactly.

    Post chain: bloom, RGB split on cuts, film grain, vignette, scanlines, colour grade. The score is synthesised in WebAudio — kick, sub-bass, hats, plucks, pads, risers and trailer impacts, arranged at 120 BPM in C minor and locked to the cut points.

    Hotkeys: space play · ←/→ step one frame · R restart · M mute · C captions · V 9:16.

    Delivery

    16:9 for YouTube pre-roll, CTV, website hero and paid social in-feed. 9:16 (press V) re-frames the same timeline for Reels, TikTok and Shorts with a brand bar and a persistent CTA.

    Captions are burned in by default — most feed views are watched with the sound off.

    Render a distributable file with node commercial/render.mjs: it steps the timeline frame-by-frame in headless Chromium, bounces the score to WAV, and muxes an H.264 MP4 with ffmpeg. Flags: --vertical, --fps 60, --no-captions, --gif.

    Everything sales-facing — brand, tagline, CTA, URL, trust badges, disclaimer, and the caption track — lives in one OTA_CONFIG block at the top of the file.
