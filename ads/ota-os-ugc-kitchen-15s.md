# OTA OS — UGC Honest-Talk Ad (15s, kitchen)

Shot-by-shot generation pack. Cuts are rendered separately and assembled, so the
**Identity Lock** block is prepended verbatim to every cut prompt — that is what keeps
the same face, hair, sweater and kitchen across all four clips.

- **Runtime:** 15s · **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical (1080x1920) · **Frame rate:** 30fps
- **Cuts:** 4 (3 jump cuts) · **Audio:** on-camera dialogue + room tone
- **Format:** smartphone UGC, phone propped on counter, no operator

---

## Identity Lock (prepend to every cut)

> Shot on a smartphone, propped on a kitchen counter, fixed handheld-style framing.
> A Black woman in her late 30s, natural hair in a loose puff, natural untouched skin
> with visible texture, pores and a few smile lines, no makeup retouching, small gold
> hoop earrings, cream knit sweater. Lived-in kitchen behind her: coffee mug on the
> counter, a child's crayon drawing taped to the fridge, an open laptop beside her
> showing a dark-green trading dashboard. Natural window light from camera left, soft
> and slightly cool. Slight sensor grain, consumer phone lens, shallow but imperfect
> focus. Calm, unhurried, dry delivery — not an ad read.

## Edit

| Beat | In | Out | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cut 1 | 0.0 | 3.5 | hard cut out on the shrug |
| Cut 2 | 3.5 | 8.0 | hard cut in mid-tap |
| Cut 3 | 8.0 | 10.5 | silent; let the mug knock land |
| Cut 4 | 10.5 | 15.0 | hold the last frame ~0.4s before end |

Straight jump cuts — no dissolves, no transitions, no music bed. Each cut changes the
camera angle slightly so the jumps read as a real person re-propping their phone.

## Audio

Dialogue recorded on-camera, phone-mic character. Room tone continuous underneath:
refrigerator hum, faint traffic. No voiceover, no music, no whoosh effects.

## Intentional flaws (keep these — they carry the UGC read)

- Light phone sensor grain throughout, heavier in the shadows
- One frame of motion blur in Cut 2 as the phone swings toward the lens
- Phone screen briefly hunting focus before it settles
- Frame a degree or two off level in Cut 1
- Soft kitchen hum left in the mix

## Negative prompt

> studio lighting, softbox, ring light, color grade, teal-and-orange, lens flare, bokeh
> background, tripod-smooth motion, gimbal, dolly move, zoom, slow motion, music, voiceover,
> stock-footage look, model-like retouched skin, smoothed skin, perfect teeth, corporate
> office, green screen, text overlays burned into the render, watermark, logo animation,
> cash, money fan, luxury car, dollar signs, rocket emoji, hype gestures, upward arrows,
> profit screenshots, percentage gains

## On-screen text

Persistent across all four cuts, bottom-safe area, small, high-contrast, no animation:

```
AI-generated ad · Education only · Not financial advice
```

Add this in the edit, not in the render — burned-in text degrades through generation and
has to stay legible and unbroken for the full 15 seconds. Keep it clear of platform UI:
bottom-safe inset ≥ 240px on 1080x1920 for TikTok and Reels.

## Compliance

The script makes no performance, profit or outcome claim and shows no gains — keep it that
way in any variant. The product is described as a decision process ("a plan, or a wait"),
not a signal service. The disclosure line is a required element of the deliverable, not an
optional overlay, and the ad is an AI-generated depiction rather than a customer testimonial.
